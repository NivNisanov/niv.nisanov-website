A peek inside

Made for parents who care

  • Calm by design

    No countdown timers and no "you lose" screens. Just gentle sounds, soft haptics and a star. It respects the silent switch, too.

  • Made for ages 2 to 5

    Big tap targets and simple cause and effect for toddlers and preschoolers. 12 mini-games for creative, learning and action play.

  • Try 4 free, unlock all 12 once

    4 games are always free. One purchase unlocks all 12 for good, with Family Sharing. No subscription and no surprise costs.

  • No ads, works offline

    No ads, ever. No Wi-Fi needed, so it is ready for the car, the plane or a waiting room.

Privacy, in plain words

Apple's App Privacy label for Tap & Play says Data Not Collected. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ad networks and no third-party SDKs. The only thing the app remembers is whether you unlocked the full game, and that stays on your device.
