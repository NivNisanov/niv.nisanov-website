TapeCue Support

Frequently asked questions

How do I add my sides?

Tap Paste sides on the home screen and paste the text, or use Import. TapeCue labels your lines (YOU) and the other character (OTHER). You can fix labels before you start.

How does the highlight follow my voice?

Turn on Live Mic. TapeCue listens as you speak and moves to the next word. If you stop, it waits for you.

Why is it not following me?

Check that microphone and speech recognition access are on in Settings > TapeCue. A quiet room helps. Speak at a natural volume.

Who reads the other lines?

A voice built into your iPhone. Pick Warm, Clear, Soft, or Bright. If a voice needs a download, TapeCue shows you how to get it in iOS Settings.

How do I record a self-tape?

Open your sides and tap Self-tape. TapeCue uses the front camera, locks orientation, and keeps a cue strip on screen. Takes save to your Photos library.

Do I need an account?

No.

Where are my sides stored?

On your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Is TapeCue on iPad?

Not right now. TapeCue is built for iPhone.

I found a bug or have an idea.

Email nlniv18@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened.
