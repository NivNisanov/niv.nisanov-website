TapeCue Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

TapeCue is developed by Niv Nisanov. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Summary

TapeCue works on your device. There is no account. We do not sell your information.

What the app uses

TapeCue requests camera, microphone, and speech recognition access.

  • Your sides. Text you paste or import is stored locally on your device. Import accepts plain text and PDF files.
  • Microphone and speech recognition. Used so the highlight can follow your voice. Speech recognition runs on your device where supported. Otherwise it is processed by Apple's speech recognition service, for example when the device or language does not support on-device recognition, or you turn off the "prefer on-device" setting.
  • Camera and microphone for self-tapes. Used only while you record.
  • Photos. Recorded takes are saved to your Photos library with your permission. Photos access is add-only and is used to save takes.
  • Voices. The other character is read by a voice built into iOS on your device.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, or contacts.
  • We do not upload your sides, audio, or video to our servers.
  • There are no third-party analytics, crash-reporting or ads SDKs.
  • TapeCue makes no network calls of its own. When speech recognition is not handled on your device, that audio is processed by Apple's speech recognition service, not by us.
  • There is no iCloud sync. Sides are stored locally on the device.

Purchases

If TapeCue offers in-app purchases, Apple handles payment. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

TapeCue is not directed to children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete sides in the app, or delete the app to remove all its data from your device. Takes saved to Photos stay in Photos until you delete them.

Changes

We will update this page if this policy changes.
