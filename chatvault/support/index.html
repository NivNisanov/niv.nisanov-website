Frequently asked questions

Why do I need screenshots? Can't ChatVault read my messages?

Apple does not let other apps read your Messages inbox. You take screenshots in Messages, and ChatVault rebuilds what is visible in them.

How do I get the best result?

Screenshot the whole thread in order, with a little overlap between shots. Keep the same text size and avoid cropping.

Which languages work?

English, Spanish, Simplified Chinese, French, German, and Japanese.

Does it work with WhatsApp or group chats?

Not yet. This version supports 1:1 Messages and SMS screenshots.

Why are some timestamps missing?

ChatVault only includes timestamps that were visible in your screenshots.

Can I fix mistakes?

Yes. Edit sender names and correct rebuilt lines before you export.

What is free and what needs Pro?

Import, rebuild, edit, and preview are free. Exporting PDF, TXT, or CSV needs ChatVault Pro (weekly or annual). There are no free exports.

How do I restore or cancel Pro?

Tap Restore Purchases on the paywall with the same Apple ID. Cancel in Settings > your name > Subscriptions.

Can I use exports as legal evidence?

ChatVault is not court-certified. Exports reflect what was visible in your screenshots. Ask a lawyer about your situation.

Where is my data?

On your device. Deleting the app removes its local data. See the Privacy Policy.

Something went wrong.

Email nlniv18@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened. Please do not send screenshots of private conversations unless you choose to.
