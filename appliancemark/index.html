Frequently asked questions

What is ApplianceMark?

ApplianceMark is an iOS app for homeowners and renters who want their appliance records in one place. You add each appliance with a purchase date, warranty length, and service notes. You can log filter changes and recurring care tasks, and you can keep a service history for each appliance. Nameplate scanning, seasonal tasks, and a privacy screen are also part of the app.

How do I keep track of appliance warranties?

Add each appliance in ApplianceMark with its purchase date, warranty length, and service notes. The warranty length stays on that appliance with the purchase date, the service notes, and the service history. You can also log filter changes and recurring care tasks for the same appliance. When something is due, ApplianceMark sends one morning reminder and shows it in a "Due today" list.

What is free in ApplianceMark?

Free in ApplianceMark costs 0 USD and covers up to 3 appliances. Pro adds unlimited appliances, smart reminders, and a PDF binder export. Pro costs 3.99 USD per month or 24.99 USD per year.

How do reminders work in ApplianceMark?

When something is due, ApplianceMark sends one morning reminder. That item appears in a "Due today" list. Pro adds smart reminders.

Does ApplianceMark run on iPhone?

Yes. ApplianceMark is an iOS app, and it runs on iPhone. You can download it from the App Store.

How do I export records from ApplianceMark?

To export records, use the PDF binder export that comes with Pro. Pro adds unlimited appliances, smart reminders, and a PDF binder export. Pro costs 3.99 USD per month or 24.99 USD per year.

What does Pro include?

Pro adds unlimited appliances, smart reminders, and a PDF binder export. Pro costs 3.99 USD per month or 24.99 USD per year. Free costs 0 USD and covers up to 3 appliances.
