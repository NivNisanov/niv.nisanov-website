Frequently asked questions

How do I use Follow the Dot?

Sit or lie comfortably and keep your head still. Let your eyes follow the dot from side to side. Breathe slowly. Tap the screen to stop anytime.

What is 4-1-6 breathing?

Breathe in for 4 counts, hold for 1, and breathe out for 6. Hypnea paces it for you. If anything feels uncomfortable, stop and breathe normally.

What is the morning check-in?

A short note about how your night felt, so you can look back on your routine.

Is Hypnea free?

Your first 2 sessions are free. After that, Hypnea Pro is $4.99 per month, $29.99 per year with a 1-week free trial, or $59.99 once for lifetime access. Prices may vary by region.

How do I cancel or restore a purchase?

Cancel in Settings > your name > Subscriptions. To restore on a new device, tap Restore Purchases on the paywall with the same Apple ID.

Is Hypnea a medical treatment?

No. Hypnea is a relaxation tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition. If you have ongoing sleep problems, talk to a doctor.

Is Hypnea safe for everyone?

If moving visuals or breathing exercises make you dizzy or uncomfortable, stop. Talk to a doctor first if you have a condition such as epilepsy or a breathing condition.
