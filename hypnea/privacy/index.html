Hypnea Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Hypnea is developed by Niv Nisanov.

Summary

Hypnea is designed to work on your device. There is no account. We do not sell your information.

What the app stores

  • Session history, morning check-ins, preferences, and onboarding answers are stored only on your device. There is no iCloud sync.
  • Purchase status comes from Apple so Pro unlocks correctly. Purchases go through Apple's App Store (StoreKit) only. Apple handles payment; we do not receive your payment details.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email, or contacts.
  • There are no third-party analytics, crash or ads SDKs.
  • Hypnea does not use Apple Health.
  • Hypnea does not use the microphone.

Permissions and network

  • Hypnea uses local notifications only (the wind-down and morning reminders), with your permission.
  • Hypnea makes no network calls other than App Store purchases. Links to research papers on the Science tab open in the browser.

Children

Hypnea is not directed to children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove its data from your device.

Changes

We will update this page if this policy changes.
